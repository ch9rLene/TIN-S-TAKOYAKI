# TIN-S-TAKOYAKI
A store owner-only order and sales management application for Tin's Takoyaki, featuring order recording, product customization, payment tracking, order status management, daily sales summaries, income monitoring, and CSV sales export.

Main features:
- Order Management – Record customer orders with flavors, sizes, spice preferences, packaging, and notes.
- Payment Tracking – Record Cash and GCash payments and monitor unpaid orders.
- Order Status – Track orders being prepared, ready for pickup, and claimed.
- Sales Dashboard – View total sales, collected payments, unpaid balances, and best-selling flavors.
- CSV Export – Export sales records for reporting.
